Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about downloading and using the PetCare app.

Where can I download the app? +

PetCare is available on Google Play for Android. The iOS version is coming soon. A direct APK is available for Android beta testers.

Is PetCare free to download and use? +

Yes, PetCare is free to download and use. [Pro plan details coming soon.]

Can I manage multiple pets of different species? +

Absolutely. PetCare supports dogs, cats, birds, horses, rabbits, and other domestic pets. You can assign each pet their own avatar, birthday, weight history, and schedule.

Will I get notifications if my phone is locked? +

Yes! PetCare uses local smart notifications scheduled right on your device. You will receive alarms for feeding times, walks, and medications even when offline.
