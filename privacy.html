Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

At PetCare, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your pet's name, breed, age, and health records. We also collect your email address if you sign in using Google or Magic Links.

2. How We Use Information

Your data is used solely to provide pet management services, including reminders, health tracking, and profile management. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We use Supabase for secure data storage and authentication. Your data is encrypted and protected using industry-standard security measures.

4. Your Rights

You can view, edit, or delete your data at any time through the app. If you delete your account, all associated pet records will be permanently removed from our servers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@petcare-app.com
